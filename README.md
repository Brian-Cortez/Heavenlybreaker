# Trial of Strength

*Trial of Strength* is a short third-person game in which the player completes a series of strength challenges inside an ancient training temple.

The player has only three primary actions:

- Punch
- Lift
- Throw

The goal is to reach the end of the temple by destroying obstacles and moving heavy objects. A complete playthrough should take approximately **5–10 minutes**.

## Gameplay

The temple contains three short challenge rooms.

### 1. Breaking Trial

Punch training dummies and cracked walls to open the exit.

### 2. Lifting Trial

Lift heavy stones and place them on pressure plates.

### 3. Throwing Trial

Pick up boulders and throw them at distant targets.

After completing all three rooms, the player earns the **Heavenly Strength Technique**. The game then awards a final score based on completion time and the number of targets destroyed.

## Magical Library

The game includes a simple magical library inspired by *Library of Heaven's Path*. The player can press a button while looking at an important object to display a hint about it.

Example:

> **Ancient Stone Wall**  
> **Flaw:** The center crack has weakened its structure.  
> **Recommendation:** Use a charged punch.

This feature only requires a small UI panel and unique text for each important object. It does not require a complicated scanning system.

## Player Abilities

- **Normal Punch:** Damages breakable objects.
- **Charged Punch:** Hold the punch button and release it to deal greater damage.
- **Lift Object:** Pick up a nearby stone, boulder, or crate.
- **Throw Object:** Throw the held object forward.
- **Library Hint:** Display information about the object currently being targeted.

## Scope

To keep the first version manageable, it will not include:

- Enemy combat
- Complicated character progression
- Inventory systems
- Multiple strength statistics
- Large levels
- Dialogue choices
- Saving and loading
- Complicated physics puzzles

The game can be built with simple cubes, rocks, walls, training dummies, and free temple assets.

## Development Goal

Create a small, complete game that can realistically be finished within **one to two weeks**. The focus is on making the three strength mechanics feel satisfying and presenting them through a short, polished sequence of challenges.
