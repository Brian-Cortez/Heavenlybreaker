Game Idea: Trial of Strength
A short third-person game where the player must complete a series of simple strength challenges inside a training temple.
The player has only three actions:
Punch
Lift
Throw
The goal is to reach the end of the temple by using strength to destroy obstacles and move heavy objects.
Simple gameplay
Create three short challenge rooms:
Breaking Trial
 Punch training dummies and cracked walls to open the exit.
Lifting Trial
 Lift heavy stones and place them on pressure plates.
Throwing Trial
 Pick up boulders and throw them at distant targets.
After completing all three rooms, the player earns the Heavenly Strength Technique and receives a final score based on completion time and destroyed targets.
Library-inspired feature
The player can press a button to open their magical library. It displays a simple hint about the object they are looking at:
Ancient Stone Wall
 Flaw: The center crack has weakened its structure.
 Recommendation: Use a charged punch.
You only need a small UI panel and different text for each important object. You do not need a complicated scanning system.
Player abilities
Normal Punch: Damages breakable objects.
Charged Punch: Hold the button and release it for greater damage.
Lift Object: Pick up a nearby stone or crate.
Throw Object: Throw the held object forward.
Library Hint: Displays information about the targeted object.
Keep the scope manageable
Avoid these features for the first version:
Enemy combat
Complicated character progression
Inventory systems
Multiple strength statistics
Large levels
Dialogue choices
Saving and loading
Complicated physics puzzles
Use simple cubes, rocks, walls, and free temple assets. The complete game should take approximately 5–10 minutes to finish.
